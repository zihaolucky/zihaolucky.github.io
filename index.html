About

More about me →

I am an AI Infrastructure & Speech-Language Systems Architect. Previously, I was a founding core AI architect of Alibaba's Tmall Genie, where I designed the NLU domain-classification engine and end-to-end ASR architecture serving tens of millions of households. Before that, I received my B.Sc. in Information and Computing Science from South China Normal University.

I am the primary author of standalone TensorBoard (dmlc/tensorboard), first inventor on two granted patents in speech and language systems, and a former Apache MXNet PMC Member and Committer. I care about building AI that operators can inspect, verify, and trust at production scale.
